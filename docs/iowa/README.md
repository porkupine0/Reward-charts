# Iowa sources for Eli's driving course

- `drivers-manual.txt`: text of the official **Iowa Driver's License Manual** (68 pages), downloaded Nov 18, 2026 from https://iowadot.gov/media/7308/download (PDF dated July 2026). Quiz questions cite its page numbers.
- **Licensing steps** (read from iowadot.gov on Nov 18, 2026):
  - **Instruction permit, from age 14:** pass the knowledge and vision test; a parent or guardian gives written consent. Drive only with adult supervision (a parent, guardian or custodian; an immediate family member 21 or older; a driver education instructor; or someone 25 or older with written parent permission). https://iowadot.gov/drivers-licenses-ids/get-or-renew-drivers-licenses-ids-permits/under-18-permit-license-or-id/instruction-permit-under-age-18
  - **Special minor's restricted license (school/work), from age 14½:** complete an Iowa-approved driver education course, and hold a valid instruction permit with a clean record for six months in a row. https://iowadot.gov/drivers-licenses-ids/get-or-renew-drivers-licenses-ids-permits/under-18-permit-license-or-id/special-minors-restricted-license
