## Summary

- All 39 questions have an answer in the manual. The intended answer is supported in every case (b = 0).
- (a) More than one choice can be defended: **2**. w1d2q4 is the real one: p41 does tell drivers to tap the brakes when followed too closely. tA14 is minor: ".08 or more" is also true for under-21 drivers.
- (b) Answer not supported by the manual: **0**.
- (c) Silly or giveaway wrong choice: **26** (7 strong: tA07, tA20, tA23, w1d1q4, w1d1q5, w1d2q5, w1d2q6; 19 mild). Most questions have at least one throwaway choice. The w1 set also tends to use the manual's exact wording as the right answer.
- (d) Confusing stem: **1** (w1d2q1, "least following distance").
- (e) Trivia: **2** (w1d1q6, the 94% statistic; tA02, octagon = stop, which is too easy; mild).
- (f) Picture: **0**. No question carries a [picture] tag. tA01, tA02, and tA03 could each be answered from a blank sign of the right color or shape.
- Duplicates or overlaps: tA19 and w1d2q1 (both ask for the 3-second rule); w1d1q2 and w1d1q7 (both about aggressive or dangerous drivers).
