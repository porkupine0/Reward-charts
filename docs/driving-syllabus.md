# Eli's driving course: 13-week plan (draft, Nov 18, 2026)

**Goal:** pass Iowa's written permit test (practice test: 25 questions, 80% to pass; the real test's length and pass mark aren't confirmed by an official page) and know the road before he drives.

## Shape of the course
- **11 units of 5 lesson days each** (days 1–4: a video lesson with a 5-question quiz; **day 5: the unit test**, about 15 questions on that unit, scored, shown on the Parents page). Lessons run Monday to Friday, one a day, so a unit is about a week; he started mid-week and holidays will shift it, so **13 weeks is the minimum**, not a date.
- **Knowledge tests** (25 questions like the real test, same topic mix every time, scored per topic, no answers shown):
  - **Baseline** (Form A): now, before lesson 1. **Built.**
  - **Progress tests** every two units: each opens once the unit test of units 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 is finished (so about every two weeks, usually a weekend). Forms B and C, alternating, so he isn't scored on questions he has already seen.
  - **Final** (Form A again, in week 13: same questions as the baseline, months later, so the two scores compare directly).
- **Weeks 12–13:** practice-test videos, Iowa DOT's own online practice test (three passes at 80% or better before he books the real one), mixed review of his missed questions, and the final test.
- **Reading his scores:** guessing alone gets about 6 of 25 right; a change of 2 or 3 questions between sittings is noise, not progress; Iowa DOT's practice test has 3 choices per question (ours have 4), so its scores run a little higher.
- **Questions:** written from the Iowa manual (every answer quoted from its page and checked by a test), on the video's topic and on what Iowa's practice test asks. I can't watch the videos (YouTube blocks automated reading), so **your preview is the check that the video matches its questions.** A "Key points" card above each video gives the manual's facts in a few lines, so a half-watched video still teaches the test material.
- **Topic weights** come from Iowa DOT's 68 practice questions: general rules (speed, turns, passing, lights) 34%, signs 15%, school buses (10%) and emergency vehicles 15%, highways/weather/road types 15%, signals and markings 7%, railroad and work zones 6%, right of way 4%, alcohol and phones 4%.

## Likely test questions
Each of Iowa DOT's 68 practice questions maps to a unit: signs, signals and the sign pictures to unit 2; markings and lane changes to 3; right of way to 4; speed, stopping, turning, signaling distances and speed limits to 5; passing, freeway entrances and merging to 6; school buses (7 questions, the most of any one topic: their own day), emergency vehicles and Move Over (3) and motorcycles to 7; railroad, work zones, parking and backing to 8; headlights, rain, hydroplaning, gravel and skids to 9; phones, alcohol and implied consent to 10; tires to 11; looking ahead, blind spots and following distance to 1.
**At least 7 have no text in the manual.** I searched the manual for these and found nothing: which way to steer in a skid, the first half-hour of rain being slickest, time being the only way to sober up, a missed freeway exit, avoiding panic stops, common crash causes, and left-turn lane position (the other stems weren't each searched). Every quiz answer must quote the manual, so the app won't ask these; the videos for those days touch some of them, and **Iowa DOT's online practice test covers them** (weeks 12–13).
**Sign pictures:** 17 of Iowa DOT's practice questions show a sign ("a sign of this shape and color means…"). The quiz needs simple drawn sign shapes (octagon, triangle, diamond, pennant, circle, pentagon, in the right colors) before unit 2 and Form B.
